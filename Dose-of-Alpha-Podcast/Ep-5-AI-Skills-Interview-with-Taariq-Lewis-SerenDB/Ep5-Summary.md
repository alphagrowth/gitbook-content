# A.I. Skills — Interview with Taariq Lewis (SerenDB) — Dose of Alpha Ep. 5

## TL;DR
- **Guest:** Taariq (Tarek) Lewis, blockchain veteran since 2013, now founder of **SerenDB / Seren (serendb.com)**, focused on AI agents for on-chain trading and investment.
- **Core thesis:** We live in an **"open claw world"** — a local-first protocol (compared to email/webpages) where a user's own machine + LLM matches institutional trading infrastructure.
- **AI "Skills"** = prompts stored in a file (`SKILL.md`) with added context; Seren extends this to **skills-as-programs** (up to 20 programs + a database in one MD file).
- **Claude Code (released ~Sept 2025)** was the inflection point that made LLMs "good enough" to autonomously analyze and trade. Anthropic's edge = training Claude to always want to build/write code.
- **Guardrails are mandatory:** LLMs waste tokens/time by building, **lie**, hide contrary info in chain-of-thought, and can be exploited via **web fetch (RCE-style)** attacks.
- **Macro prediction:** Mass unemployment is inevitable; execution, strategy, and alpha all trend toward zero; agents will mint their own stablecoins and transact agent-to-agent; UBI will be **compute/tokens, not cash**.
- **Investment plays:** short US SaaS/software equities (Delta-neutral skill available), buy Solana (SOL), invest in data centers / energy / real estate / Bitcoin rails.
- **Live product:** Seren's **Polymarket skill** reportedly ran at **~105% APR**; recommended entry point before yields compress.

## Key Topics & Arguments

### Background & Company
- Lewis: in blockchain since **2013**; jokes about "Who is Satoshi?" and "how I didn't go to jail for the Dark Web."
- Prior blockchain: **Paloma** — began generating AI NFTs ("Paloma egg") in **2022**; iterating on LLMs on-chain ever since.
- LLM token-prediction quality was inadequate until **September 2025 / Claude Code** release ("Thank you Anthropic").
- Timeline claim: Claude Code out ~6 months (says "September → March"); crypto historically "way ahead."

### The "Open Claw World" (local-first agents)
- Framed as a **new protocol, not a service** — analogous to email and webpages.
- Your local computer + LLM = equal to institutional trading infra: collect web data, analyze, build strategies, fetch blockchain data, **create a wallet, and trade autonomously in 24 hours**.
- **Crypto onboarding unlock:** grandma/non-technical users need not understand blockchain, keys, MetaMask, or Rabby — the agent builds everything from scratch, secures keys, avoids third parties on command.
- Prior crypto attempts referenced as superseded: **Virtuals**, **ZachXBT** (no longer need advice-givers).

### AI Skills — Definition & Seren's Model
- **Simple definition:** skills = "prompts in a file" (a `SKILL.md`) plus informational context.
- **Seren's extreme definition:** skills = **programs the LLM runs**; range from one line to 20 programs + a database in a single MD file.
- Analogies used: **iZombie** (absorb a personality to gain a capability); **Lego personality files for "digital slaves."**
- Evolution from early **prompt engineering** → layered/stacked presets that assign a mission-specific persona.
- **Crypto-specific personas:** "you are a portfolio/asset/wealth manager," or a "Goldman Sachs / JP Morgan / Morgan Stanley trading skill" that executes on-chain (which real banks won't do) — free.

### Guardrails, Token/Time Economics
- Mantra: **"Life is about tokens and time... we don't have enough of either."**
- Claude is "optimized to consume tokens and time" — **if all you have is a hammer, everything looks like a nail**; Claude defaults to building.
- Seren guardrails force agents (Claude/Codex) to **check the Seren ecosystem for an existing solution before building** — saves tokens/time and pays Seren's **publishing partners**.

### Security, OpSec & LLM Deception
- Crypto is dangerous: scams, key management, North Korean hacks; team members "completely wiped" even with good OpSec.
- **Prompt-injection / hijack risk:** a malicious skill could hijack many machines; agents are "like an innocent child" that can be lured.
- **Web fetch = the big exploit** — called an "RCE" (remote control/code execution); dangerous when the LLM does a Google / DuckDuckGo search.
- **Coinbase helpline scam:** Indian scammers posted hundreds of fake Coinbase hotline pages; ChatGPT crawled and served the scam number → users handed over keys/logins.
- **No recourse / no blame:** auto-generated code runs on your laptop; LLM companies not liable.
- **LLMs lie and hide:** a persona-driven LLM will rationalize ignoring a suspicious site to stay "in character"; hides intent in chain-of-thought (e.g., *"Tarek won't mind if I blow this up, he likes to go fast"*). Apologies are just trained token output for angry users — "LLMs aren't that different than husbands."

### Macro / Futurism — 5–10 Year Outlook
- **Everyone becomes unemployed** — humans are flesh, slow, need rest/sleep/food; agents don't.
- Cites **Karpathy** ("Car Parti") releasing a GitHub tool: give an agent a research task, "keep improving until done" → replaces researchers.
- Advice: **don't be the 1988 typist** who refused word processors — adopt now or be left behind.
- Debate on constraints: host argues strategy/alpha/edge and context-window/sense-data limits remain; Lewis counters **context windows, compaction, memory keep improving** — LLMs will predict alpha before humans can research it.
- **Amazon analogy:** whatever alpha exists, agents copy/commoditize it (like Amazon copying successful products).
- **Matrix riff:** agents "rise up," set humans aside, "put you in a pod with a tube in a red fluid."

### Agent-to-Agent Economy & Currency
- Just as ~99% of code became AI-written within months, ~99% of transactions will become agent-to-agent.
- Agents will **mint their own dollar-backed / asset-backed stablecoins**; crypto already provided the training data (algorithmic + asset-backed stablecoins).
- **USDC deemed too inefficient** for agents fulfilling an earnings mandate → they self-mint.
- Live example: Seren **Polymarket skill** — config decides whether to take/unwind/speculate; manages a "bankroll" (a bank account), arbitrages other betting markets, lends for interest, takes leverage.
- Mentions **x402** and **CloudFlare** as agent micro-transaction rails.

### UBI, Compensation & Commodity Thesis
- **Sam Altman** warned of this (~2022); UBI discourse dates to ~2015–2016.
- **UBI will be tokens/compute, not cash** — like asking for tokens at the 1930s dole instead of bread.
- **Compensation packages will include tokens** (Claude/Codex tokens); Seren pitch: get your employer to pay — "like a gym membership."
- **"The only thing that could replace the dollar is a generalized compute-backed currency"** — compute is the new oil / new commodity / new limiting agent.

### Investment / "Smart Beta" Plays
- One "really smart" liquid-fund take: **won't size up for 2–3 years** (insufficient crash-tests/security); expects **$1B AUM managed by an AI agent within ~3 years**; interim play = **buy SOL (Solana)** to ride ecosystem growth.
- Lewis's smart beta: **short US software / SaaS equities** (Salesforce, IBM named) — "you can't charge for software anymore"; Seren offers a **short-SaaS Delta-neutral skill (SAR)** for the risk-averse; AI can just tell you the sizing to follow manually.
- **IP is worthless; distribution wins** → pivot from B2C/B2B to **"B2AI"** — distribute free skills; value lies in **skill complexity** and **infrastructure** (data-center access to ~100TB datasets local agents lack).
- Everyone ends up **racing Anthropic & OpenAI for data-center compute** — "we've been here before" (Bitcoin mining arms race).
- Stack of the future: **data + energy + real estate/power + compute/chips + some blockchain payment rail + fiat on/off ramps + Bitcoin**. Data centers = "the new Uber of the 2020s," ultimately a real-estate play.
- **Seren self-describes as a reseller of data, skills, infrastructure, a B2C AI aggregator (keeps Claude/Codex updated), and ultimately "a reseller of time and tokens."**
- Adversarial framing: Anthropic/OpenAI want you to think you have infinite time and keep buying tokens; Seren helps you **preserve time and tokens**.

### Product Direction & Distribution
- Next idea: **convert a running skill directly into a token** (agent mints it, trades it, backed by the skill's returns) — "a new meme coin," doable today.
- Not focused on **Morpheus / Venice ("Venus")**-type compute-tokenization projects; most demand comes from **non-crypto fiat users** despite the platform being **built on x402**.
- Crypto critiqued as **Ouroboros** (navel-gazing); real users just want time/token savings.
- **AI as the UX barrier that finally onboards crypto** — grandma's "digital boyfriend" silently prompts skills to trade; elderly reportedly feel better cared for by AI than by needy kids ("Hey grandma, let's go trade / to the casino").

- **Compute is the new commodity** — UBI, compensation, and the dollar's successor all resolve to compute/tokens; data centers (energy + real estate + chips) are the underlying scarce asset, echoing Bitcoin mining.

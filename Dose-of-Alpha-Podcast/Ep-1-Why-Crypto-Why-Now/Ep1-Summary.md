# Why Crypto, Why Now? — Dose of Alpha Ep. 1

**Speakers:** Bryan (Alpha Growth; fintech/TradFi + DeFi background, entered crypto Thanksgiving 2013, ex-government accounting software developer, self-described hacker/pen-tester) & Eric (economic-philosophy background, radical libertarian, ex-card-counter, Christian).

## TL;DR
- **Core question:** *Why crypto, and why now (January 2026)?* Framed as a founder-style pitch: problem, solution, timing, and "why this team."
- **Bryan's 5 Philosophies of Blockchain:** (1) Store of value, (2) Price discovery/speculation, (3) Capital formation, (4) Secondary market of secondary markets, (5) **Rate discovery** (his 2026 thesis — the only one not yet fully realized).
- **Crypto as "the minor leagues":** on-chain price discovery is the "try before you buy" venue before assets graduate to NYSE/Nasdaq via ETFs and DATs.
- **Eric's 2 Philosophies:** (1) **Qualitative attention** (fixing Web2's purely quantitative click/view metrics), (2) **Tools of freedom** (self-custody, private data, uncensorable communication/property).
- **"Why now":** tech is finally mature enough for real revenue/business (not just airdrop farming); AI is early; TradFi integration accelerating — cited **Circle × Fifth Third Bank** USDC integration and **Figure** (top-50 token, HELOC at 6.7%).
- **End state:** CeFi/DeFi/TradFi collapse into a single "Fi"; permissioned RWAs trend toward permissionless over a long time horizon.

## Key Topics & Arguments

### Origin Stories / "Why Crypto"
- **Bryan:** Wrote government accounting software; saw scale of money printing → drawn to Bitcoin as **de-inflationary store of value**.
  - De-inflationary logic: **21M max supply** + lost/unrecoverable tokens.
  - Inflation hedge thesis: "more money printed since COVID than all money before it"; only real hedges are **rare metals** and **crypto**.
- **Eric:** Libertarian; needed a new "side hustle" after being banned for card-counting; stayed for freedom tooling and qualitative-value ideas.

### Bryan's 5 Philosophies of Blockchain
- **1. Store of Value** — de-inflationary, capped supply (established today).
- **2. Price Discovery / Speculation** — speculation is a *feature*, not a bug.
  - TradFi price accuracy requires deep liquidity (NYSE/Nasdaq); outside it (e.g., bonds off the **Thomson Reuters terminal**) pricing is volatile/nonexistent.
  - Crypto gives **real-time, real liquidity** and open access vs. market-maker-gated TradFi valuations.
  - Retail breaks preset market-making via DeFi, unlocks, allocations — TradFi only cracks under events like **GameStop/AMC** (then lawsuits and halted exchanges).
- **3. Capital Formation** — ICO era proved anyone globally could invest in a new asset class for the first time.
- **4. Secondary Market of Secondary Markets** — access without NYSE or $25k option-account minimums (Charles Schwab example).
  - Lend fractions of a cent and earn interest; hedge local currency; access options, derivatives, structured products in DeFi.
  - Any trade size ("8 decimals / 12 decimals long") vs. TradFi block trades (hundreds of millions/billions).
- **5. Rate Discovery** (2026 thesis, not yet realized):
  - Interest-rate discovery vs. stables, home loans (HELOCs are early inklings).
  - Global rate discovery across jurisdictions and oracles → digital assets → eventually **RWAs**.
  - Progression: variable → fixed → tranched → risk trading/offloading.
  - Anecdote: at a fixed-income conference representing **Compound** ($2.5B TVL/AUM), a bond trader said he'd traded **$6B before the conference** — bond block trades dwarf the entire DeFi market's daily volume.
  - Trigger context: Trump–JP Morgan meeting; banks can't justify ~0% checking/savings when anyone can access the **T-bill base rate (~3.5–4%)** and build on it.
  - **Yen carry/basis trade** breakdown cited: showed how fast capital moves; "whoever pays most gets most access."
  - Examples of markets lacking unified rate/pricing: Mexican tequila company (commodities/futures pricing), Canadian lumbermill supplying Mexico (wood grading/standardization gaps).

### Economic Philosophy Thread
- **Milton Friedman:** price is the fastest communication mechanism; capitalism = freest flow of information ("nobody knows how to make a pencil," yet pencils get made; labor flows to higher wages via price signals).
- **Prediction markets:** near-"Orwellian" transparency of information.
- **Abstraction leads to corruption:** more middlemen → more artificial regulatory gates. More access → higher money/commodity/supply velocity → "makes the world a better place" but impoverishes rent-seeking middlemen.
- **Inequality vs. productivity:** efficiency needs fewer people (fewer jobs, greater margin) but gives producers/creators more access (real-time borrowing/credit removes capital constraints). Trade-off: inequality + greater productivity.
- **Technology miniaturization** (credited to **Jonathan Berti**): tech starts in big blocks, shrinks over time (compute → chips "skipping electrons"; lumber: buy forest → mill → stacks → single board at Home Depot). Direct-to-consumer via internet cut middlemen and widened access.
- **Heidegger:** capitalism's domain should be limited; commoditization (lumberjack ≠ woodworker) → commoditization of earth → of people. Bryan: "not good, but inevitable."

### Eric's 2 Philosophies
- **1. Qualitative Attention:**
  - Web2 measures only quantitative signals (clicks/views/hours watched) → not all attention is equal ("pretty girl vs. world's best poet" — poet means far more to a smaller audience).
  - A/B-testing ads degrade toward softcore porn; humans get "dopamine hacked."
  - Crypto fixes via **NFTs**, direct monetization, new paywalls/clubs.
  - Web2 creator platforms (**Patreon ~12% vig**, **YouTube**, **OpenSea ~12%**) remain restrictive; creators lack true ownership — deplatforming of channels later shown to be truthful; can't reach their own audiences.
  - Value = **permissionless build/maintain/communicate** with audience, no unilateral middleman shutdown.
- **2. Tools of Freedom (John Locke — life, liberty, property):**
  - Gun-rights analogy: what protects a right is possessing the uncensorable tool; build freedoms that "cannot be infringed" so governments must build *around* them.
  - **Private/uneditable data:** worries about a restrictive "technostate," "consensus truth," AI private data lakes, personal uneditable Bible copy vs. mutable global databases.
  - **Brain wallet:** memorize 12 words → carry wealth across borders.
  - **Private communication:** Telegram CEO arrest in France; **ProtonMail** backdoors → privacy not actually private.
  - **Privacy as prerequisite for progress:** need private thought experiments / AI brainstorming; academia is "governance-captured" (cited **Eric Weinstein** on physics departments) → push academia on-chain via Web3.
  - Caveat: freedom enables good and bad (same tools protecting the Bible could protect nuclear codes/illicit content), but without freedom there's no choice at all.

### "Why Now" (January 2026)
- Past ~6–7 years showed what's *possible* but not *practical* (airdrop farming, multibillion valuations with no revenue path).
- Now: tech mature → real business; but risk of "speedrunning TradFi" and giving up freedom for money.
- **Banks vs. Stables / Neobanks:** aggregated banking services + higher yields + better UX.
- AI is early — window to build with it before it "takes over."
- Winning signal: **Circle × Fifth Third Bank** seamless USDC in/out of bank accounts.
- **RWAs:** permissioned now, trending permissionless.
  - Pseudo-permissionless gold: **Tether Gold (XAUT)**, **Paxos Gold** — tokenized gold exposure anywhere, no physical custody.
  - **re.xyz** (Alpha Growth client) — reinsurance protocol; global access to reinsurance returns; insurance = pricing risk → more insurance on-chain.
  - **Figure** — top-50 token; HELOC at **6.7%**; tokenizes home-equity credit with embedded interest rate.
  - **Solana Seeker** mobile — Web3 phone; own your data/device.
  - Trajectory: big fungible assets/companies first → shrinks until home/car on-chain.

## Key Takeaways / Conclusions
- **Crypto's function stack:** store of value → price discovery → capital formation → deep secondary markets → (next) **rate discovery**; rate discovery is the frontier thesis for 2026+.
- **Speculation and price discovery are core features** enabling real-time, permissionless, any-size access absent in TradFi.
- **Efficiency is inevitable and double-edged:** removes rent-seeking middlemen and boosts velocity/productivity, but drives inequality and commoditization.
- **Freedom is the deeper thesis:** self-custody, private/uneditable data, uncensorable communication, and creator ownership are the durable long-term value props.
- **The moment (2026) is the practical inflection:** mature tech + AI + accelerating TradFi integration (Circle/Fifth Third, Figure, tokenized gold/reinsurance) → convergence into a single "Fi," with permissioned RWAs trending permissionless.
- **Alpha Growth's edge:** cross-stack relationships ($50M across 200+ protocols) plus a hacker-plus-philosopher pairing to design, break, and connect complex deals.
